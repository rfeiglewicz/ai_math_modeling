                          BF16 EXP2 / EXPE HARDWARE ARCHITECTURE
                              WIDTH-OPTIMISED CORE (optim)
                          ====================================

Companion to exp_fpga_schematic.txt, which documents the production core. This
one documents bf16_exp2_optim / bf16_exp2_optim_approx(), the minimum-width
datapath that stays BIT-IDENTICAL to bf16_exp2_approx<29> over all 65536 BF16
patterns in both modes.

Widths come from bf16_exp2_optim_pkg.sv, which mirrors namespace bf16_optim_cfg
in bf16_exp2_optim.hpp one-for-one. Production width in [brackets] where it
differs.

Measured on xc7a200tfbg484-1, 15 pipeline stages, ROUND_MODE=1 (half-up):
368 LUT, 14 CARRY4, 272 FF, 2 DSP, 0.5 BRAM36, 177.5 MHz.
Same depth production core: 654 LUT, 33 CARRY4, 506 FF, 5 DSP, 161.7 MHz.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE THE SAVINGS COME FROM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Rounding the log2(e) product to Q2.21 immediately, instead of carrying Q2.29
   forward. 21 is the RNE minimum -- 20 breaks base-e. Plain truncation needs
   22, which is unreachable because MANT_MULT_F is the module's own output
   field width. That is why the core rounds rather than truncates.

2. UNIFIED_F = MANT_MULT_F = 21, so FRAC_PAD = 0. The unified register drops
   from 47 to 30 bits and the barrel shifter shrinks with it.

3. x truncated to 17 bits, so both multiplier operands fit one DSP48E1.

4. Dropping the dead integer bit from a and b. Over the whole input space
   a is in [0.347983, 0.690553] and b is in [0.847980, 0.999993], so neither
   ever reaches 1.0 and the Q1.20 integer bit is dead weight. ROM word goes
   from 42 to 35 bits, which is what puts it in half a BRAM36.

5. b - a*x is in [0.5, 1) for every reachable input, so the accumulator is
   unsigned Q0.18: no sign bit, no integer guard bits, and normalisation
   collapses to a bit test and a 2:1 mux.

Points 4 and 5 are properties of THIS table over THIS input space, not
identities. They are re-verified exhaustively by tests/exp_pwl_optim.cpp and by
make rtl_optim_static_norm rather than being taken on trust.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROUND_MODE AND THE CRITICAL PATH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RNE and half-up differ only on an exact tie. With LOG2E_VAL = 0x5c551d there is
exactly ONE tie in the entire input space: mant_src = 0x80, i.e. inputs of the
form +/-2^E, and only on the base-e path. The resulting 1 ULP at 2^-21 does not
survive the final rounding to 7 mantissa bits, so both modes are bit-exact
against bf16_exp2_approx.

That equality is empirical, not an identity -- the width sweep fails at 20 bits
for both modes, so the margin carrying it is about one bit. half-up therefore
gets its own exhaustive run (make rtl_optim_half_up) instead of inheriting the
RNE result.

The choice is free at the output and not free in timing. With retiming maxed
out (make sweep_optim, xc7a200t):

    ROUND_MODE=0 RNE      164.8 MHz, 8 levels, critical path in u_log2e_mult
    ROUND_MODE=1 half-up  177.5 MHz, 5 levels, critical path in u_round

for 369 vs 368 LUT. The difference is the sticky OR and the lsb term feeding
the rounding adder's carry-in; half-up needs only the guard bit.
